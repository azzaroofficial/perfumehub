House of Azzaro

BORN UNDER THE MEDITERRANEAN SUN

Founded in Paris by Tunisian-Sicilian designer Loris Azzaro, our fragrance house has celebrated hedonism, high glamour, and timeless seduction for nearly half a century.

Every bottle is an architectural marvel — from the iconic mechanical revolver-barrel cylinder of Azzaro Wanted to the radiant, pure glass flacon of Chrome. We sustainably harvest rare botanicals, Bourbon vanilla from Madagascar, and noble woods to craft sillage that commands presence.
