Everything You Need To Know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are all perfumes 100% authentic Azzaro Paris creations?

Yes, unconditionally. Every bottle sold via our boutique is 100% genuine, imported directly from Paris in original luxury retail packaging with verified batch codes printed on the bottom of the glass flacon and outer box.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours from our climate-controlled depot. Express courier delivery takes 2 to 4 business days worldwide. For Sri Lanka and local metropolitan regions, delivery is completed within 24 to 48 hours.

How do I store my Azzaro perfume to preserve maximum longevity?

Keep your perfume flacon away from direct sunlight, high humidity, and extreme temperature fluctuations (avoid storing in hot bathrooms). Storing the bottle in its original box or a dark vanity closet preserves the delicate top notes and sillage for up to 5+ years.

Can I test scents before purchasing a full bottle?

Yes! Every order of any full flacon automatically includes two complimentary 2ml luxury spray discovery vials of your choice at checkout so you can explore other creations risk-free.
