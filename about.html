The Founder

LORIS AZZARO & THE SPIRIT OF HEDONISM

Tunisian-born of Sicilian descent, Loris Azzaro envisioned a world of eternal celebration. In 1968, he founded his Paris couture fashion house, quickly dressing international icons from Sophia Loren to Brigitte Bardot.

In 1978, he unveiled Azzaro Pour Homme — an instant global triumph formulated with an unheard-of 320 raw essences. It established the house as a vanguard of masculine seduction and Mediterranean vitality.

"All my senses are seduced by the sun, the sea, and the fragrance of orange blossoms."

— Loris Azzaro
